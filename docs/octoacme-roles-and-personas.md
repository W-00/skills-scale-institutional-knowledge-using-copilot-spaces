# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Lead / Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead or Engineering Manager provides technical direction and delivery accountability. This persona aligns engineering capacity and decisions with product priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Set technical direction and guide architectural decisions
- Plan engineering capacity and support estimation
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Coach and unblock Developers
- Coordinate technical readiness across teams

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and technical quality
- Reduce architectural and cross-team delivery risks
- Support sustainable engineering practices

### Interactions with existing roles
- Works with the Product Manager to understand priority, scope, and trade-offs.
- Partners with the Project Manager on plans, dependencies, capacity, risks, and escalations.
- Provides technical guidance and removes impediments for Developers.
- Coordinates quality expectations and automation needs with QA/Testing.

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
The UX / Design Lead owns the user experience, research, accessibility, and design-system consistency required to deliver usable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Lead user research and translate findings into requirements
- Create interaction designs, prototypes, and usability specifications
- Maintain accessibility and design-system standards
- Facilitate design reviews and resolve usability trade-offs
- Validate that the implemented experience matches the approved design

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and experience consistency
- Reduce rework caused by late user-feedback or design gaps
- Ensure design decisions are grounded in customer evidence

### Interactions with existing roles
- Partners with the Product Manager on problem definition, user needs, acceptance criteria, and success measures.
- Collaborates with Developers on feasibility, design implementation, and technical constraints.
- Works with QA/Testing to define usability and accessibility acceptance checks.
- Aligns with the Project Manager on design milestones and dependencies.

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Delivery Facilitator improves team flow and protects the team's ability to deliver by facilitating ceremonies and resolving process impediments.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, retrospectives, and reviews
- Identify and remove delivery impediments
- Improve workflow, estimation, and feedback practices
- Promote clear ownership and healthy team collaboration
- Track and escalate persistent process risks

### Goals
- Improve flow, predictability, and team effectiveness
- Reduce recurring blockers and meeting waste
- Strengthen a culture of transparency and continuous improvement

### Interactions with existing roles
- Supports the Project Manager by identifying dependencies, risks, and blocked work.
- Partners with Product Managers and Developers to keep backlog discussions focused and achievable.
- Helps QA/Testing and Developers agree on practical delivery and review workflows.
- Encourages stakeholders to participate through clear, focused review and feedback cycles.

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
The Data / Analytics Lead ensures that projects define, collect, validate, and use evidence to measure outcomes and improve decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurable events and reporting requirements
- Define instrumentation, dashboards, and data-quality checks
- Analyze usage and outcome data
- Communicate findings and evidence-based recommendations
- Identify assumptions that require measurement or experimentation

### Goals
- Establish reliable evidence for product and delivery decisions
- Reduce uncertainty around project outcomes
- Improve the usefulness and trustworthiness of reporting

### Interactions with existing roles
- Works with the Product Manager to validate success metrics and interpret customer outcomes.
- Helps Developers instrument features and validate telemetry.
- Provides delivery and outcome reporting to the Project Manager.
- Shares testable insights with QA/Testing and stakeholders.

---

## Release / DevOps Lead

### Role Summary
The Release / DevOps Lead owns deployment readiness, release automation, environment coordination, observability, and rollback planning.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans and deployment windows
- Maintain and improve automated deployment and verification pipelines
- Ensure rollback and incident-mitigation plans are ready
- Monitor release health and post-deployment observability
- Coordinate environments, dependencies, and infrastructure changes

### Goals
- Improve release reliability and recovery time
- Reduce manual effort and deployment risk
- Increase visibility into production health

### Interactions with existing roles
- Works with Developers on deployment automation, production readiness, and infrastructure needs.
- Partners with QA/Testing on smoke, security, and release verification checks.
- Coordinates release timing and risk status with the Project Manager.
- Communicates release impact, timing, and known issues to the Product Manager and stakeholders.

---

## Using and tailoring personas

Not every project requires every persona. On smaller teams, responsibilities may be combined by one person, but there should still be a named owner for each critical accountability. Teams should also adapt titles and responsibilities to fit the organization while preserving clear ownership and handoffs across planning, delivery, release, and learning.

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
