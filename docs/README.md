# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A practical guide to how OctoAcme initiates, plans, executes, releases, and improves projects. OctoAcme emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychologically safe collaboration.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme follows a project lifecycle that begins by validating a business need, identifying stakeholders, defining measurable success criteria, and deciding whether to proceed. Approved initiatives are planned into prioritized backlog items with acceptance criteria, estimates, a Definition of Done, milestones, and identified risks and dependencies.

During execution, the team tracks work on a project board and coordinates through regular standups, delivery syncs, planning, and demos. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; the Product Manager owns outcomes and prioritization; developers build and test changes; and QA validates quality and acceptance criteria. Blockers are escalated from the team to the PM, Product Lead, and Sponsor as appropriate.

Quality assurance is built into delivery through unit and integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, security scanning, manual QA when needed, and CI checks for tests and linting before review. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and a rollback or mitigation plan. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, teams capture learnings and track a small number of actionable improvements.
